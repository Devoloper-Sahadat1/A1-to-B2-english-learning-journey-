# 🇺🇸🚀 A1 → B2 American English Journey
### Complete 80-topic roadmap + reusable AI Master Prompt

> **Goal:** Build practical English ability from CEFR A1 → A2 → B1 → B2 through focused topics and interactive AI tutoring.

---

## 🇺🇸 American English Standard

This entire curriculum follows **American English**.

Use American English for:
- spelling (`color`, `favorite`, `center`)
- vocabulary (`apartment`, `elevator`, `vacation`, `gas`)
- pronunciation and speaking models
- grammar and usage examples
- listening materials and recommended resources
- natural everyday expressions

When British and American English differ, **teach American English as the default**. British English may be mentioned only when useful for comparison.

---

## 📌 How to Use This README

Use **one topic per AI chat**.

For every topic:

1. Open a new AI chat.
2. Copy the **Master Prompt** from the bottom of this file.
3. Replace `[TOPIC NAME]` with the topic.
4. Study interactively: **Teach → Practice → Correct → Repeat → Increase difficulty**.
5. Review after **1 day → 7 days → 30 days**.
6. Move on when you can actually use the topic, not merely recognize it.

### Core learning cycle

```text
Understand → Vocabulary → Sentences → Grammar → Listening
→ Reading → Speaking → Writing → Review → Test
```

# 🇬🇧 + 🇧🇩 English & Bangla Support

Because the learner starts at A1, use **English-first + Bangla-support**.

| Level | Suggested balance |
|---|---|
| A1 | English 60% + Bangla 40% |
| A1+/A2 | English 70% + Bangla 30% |
| B1 | English 85% + Bangla 15% |
| B2 | English 95% + Bangla 5% |

These are flexible guidelines.

For important new vocabulary, give:

1. English word
2. Part of speech
3. Simple English meaning
4. Bangla meaning
5. Natural example
6. Common collocations
7. Practice question

If I do not understand a sentence:

```text
Original English
↓
Bangla meaning
↓
Difficult vocabulary
↓
Simple English
↓
Practice
```

Do **not** translate everything automatically. The long-term goal is:

```text
English → Meaning
```

rather than:

```text
English → Bangla → Meaning
```

If I answer in Bangla because I do not know how to express something, convert my idea into natural English, explain the key phrases, and make me say it again in English.
